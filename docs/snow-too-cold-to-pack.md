# Snow too cold to pack

The image behind "being surprised by what emerges" in the spec's "Why bother" bullet (under [Intent and context](../Off%20the%20Record%20DevTalk%20Retro%20Spec.md#intent-and-context)).

## The story

The day before the talk, I tried to make a snowman, but the snow was too cold to stick together. I'd heard as a kid that the fix is to keep tossing shovelfuls into the pile as hard as you can, but I'd never seen anyone patient enough to actually find out. So I did. All that energy had nowhere to go but into the snow, so it had to melt a little, and it finally packed.

Then I started imagining what you could do with a whole field of it: a blanket of material that shows up every year and melts away mostly unused.

## What it has to do with the retro

The retro is the shovel. A room of very different people each throw in something they felt this year, and with nowhere else for it to go, some of it sticks together into themes nobody planned. What comes out depends on how different the people are, which is why a room full of different vantage points matters more than a room full of experts.

A year of feelings across a community is like that field: it shows up every year, and most of it melts away unsaid. The retro is one small try at finding out what it could become if some of it got packed together for an evening.
