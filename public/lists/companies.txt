# naming the university or polytechnic doesn't identify anyone.
# One name per line, matched case-insensitively as whole words.
# Matching is literal: "federated co-op" does not match "federated co-operatives" or "coop",
# so list each spelling people actually write. Possessives ("Nutrien's") match on their own.
# Leave out names that are everyday or tech words ("spaces", "opus", "toast", "sha", "sed"):
# a hint that fires on ordinary sentences teaches people to ignore it.
# DevTalk(s) is left out too: the retro runs at a DevTalk, so it would fire on harmless cards.
